Delete your PaperTrade account

You can ask us to permanently delete your PaperTrade account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to jingalalahu4890@gmail.com with the subject PaperTrade account deletion.
  2. Send it from the email address registered on your account (so we can verify it is you).
  3. We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, display name, password hash, risk profile)
  • Your virtual portfolio, orders, trades, watchlists, alerts, notes, lesson progress, streaks and notification inbox
  • Your league memberships and leaderboard entries

What may be kept

Limited records we must keep for legal, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons, such as purchase records (product ID and order state), kept only as long as required. In-app purchases are managed by Google Play: deleting your account does not cancel or refund a purchase; request refunds through Google Play.
