PaperTrade Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

PaperTrade ("the app", "we") is a stock-trading simulator for education. It uses virtual money only. It is not a broker, does not place real trades, and does not provide investment advice. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy
Account data: email address, display name (optional, does not have to be unique), password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text), and the risk profile you choose at sign-up.To create and secure your account and sign you in.
App activity you create: virtual portfolio, orders and trades, watchlists, price alerts, notes and tags, lesson progress, streaks, achievements, league membership, and your simulated performance statistics.To provide the simulator, your analytics and insights, and leaderboards.
Notification inbox: notifications generated for you (for example order fills or alert triggers).To show you events in the app and, if you allow notifications, as on-device notifications.
Purchase information: the product ID, order state and purchase token of in-app products you buy. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.To unlock what you bought (such as ad removal), restore purchases, and handle refunds.
Ad and device data collected by Google AdMob, such as advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, when ads are shown.To show and measure ads. See section 3.
Basic server logs: IP address, request time and technical details.Security, abuse prevention and fixing errors.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not currently use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. What other users can see

If you appear on a leaderboard or join a private league, other users can see your display name and your simulated performance (for example return and rank). They cannot see your email, notes or holdings. You can use a display name that does not identify you.

3. Advertising and consent

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising ID and similar data to serve and measure ads, and may personalise ads where permitted. Where required (for example the EEA, UK and some US states) the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later under Profile > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying the "Remove ads" product stops banner and interstitial ads.

4. Service providers

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app, and we do not sell your personal data:

  • Google (Google Play, Play Billing and AdMob) for purchases and ads.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the app's database and runs our servers.
  • Finnhub, our market-data provider. We send it stock symbols to fetch prices; we do not send it any personal data.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the app and its users.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time (see how to delete your account). We delete it within 30 days, except limited records we must keep for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons (for example purchase records).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

PaperTrade is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Educational use only

PaperTrade is a simulator. Prices may be simulated or delayed, and simulated results do not predict real results. Nothing in the app is investment, financial, tax or legal advice.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change and, for significant changes, we will notify you in the app.
